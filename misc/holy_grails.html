My Holy Grails

A collection of intellectual achievements that resonate particularly well with me.

I list these to keep a log of my interests and to suggest a unifying pattern in the kinds of results I eventually want my own work to achieve.

  1. 01

    Gödel's incompleteness proofs

    Notes
    Shattering the illusion of perfect formalisms... what a lovely result
  2. 02

    The Curry–Howard correspondence

    Notes
    I was introduced to this upon starting PL research, and it was extremely satisfying to me. I guess I love when formal objects like "programs" and "proofs" (which are already a lot to thing about: complex, fundamental, and offering infinite unthought future instances) elegantly collapse in on each other by equivalence, thereby making the world a little simpler and more amenable to analysis.
  3. 03

    Lacanian psychoanalysis

    Notes
    The whole framework. Listening to lectures on Lacanian psychoanalysis is in itself a form of therapy for me. To put it in a way I may not be licensed to yet (one day), the field is full of so many delicious inversions that it seems to yield insights every time I engage with it (or maybe I just never learn the first time).
