Projects

Pairs Trading With GMM-MinHash Regime Signaling

Original pairs trading strategy developed with advanced data science techniques. Apply Gaussian Mixture Models for regime signaling and MinHash for historical return predictions to provide combined trade signals on cointegrated equity pairs in the S&P 500.

GitHub Repo | Report

SMT-Based Cross-Currency Arbitrage Detection

A Z3 SMT-based cross-currency arbitrage detector meant as a more expressive alternative to Bellman-Ford. Extended to model fees, nonlinear slippage, and integer lot sizes, revealing capital-dependent arbitrage opportunities missed by traditional graph-based methods.

GitHub Repo

Featured Algorithmic Trading SMT Solving Z3 Python Optimization

Zeroes of the Riemann Zeta Function and Spectral Statistics of Random Matrices

Complex variables report co-authored with Andrew Yang and Ari Clark. Explains properties of Riemann zeta function through complex analytic methods and explores Gaussian and Circular Unitary Ensembles from random matrix theory as an approach towards solving the Riemann Hypothesis.

Experimental physics research collaboration with NLR (formerly NREL) studying the degradation of perovskite solar cells under stress.
